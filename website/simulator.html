Interactive anchoring simulator

A radiologist's diagnostic accuracy depends not only on their own skill and the AI's accuracy, but on how much weight they place on the AI's suggestion. Drag the slider to change the anchoring weight (wAI) and watch the simulated cohort accuracy update in real time.

Adjust the AI deference weight

wAI = 0 — clinician ignores the AI entirely. wAI = 1 — clinician fully defers to the AI. Empirical estimates from Gaube et al. (2021) suggest deployed clinicians sit around wAI ≈ 0.45.

What the simulator is doing

For each of the 234 simulated studies in the validation set, the clinician has an internal probability estimate for the positive class (pclin) calibrated to a baseline accuracy of 0.86. The AI produces its own probability (pAI), sampled to match the per-pathology AUCs of Irvin et al. (2019). Following Gaube et al. (2021) we model the posterior as a log-odds blend:

logit(ppost) = (1 − wAI) · logit(pclin) + wAI · logit(pAI)

The clinician's final decision is 1[ppost ≥ 0.5]. Sweeping wAI from 0 to 1 produces the curves above — a behavioural decomposition of the human-AI hand-off.
